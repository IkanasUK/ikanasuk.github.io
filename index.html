About Us

One Alma Mater,
One Community

IKANAS UK is the UK chapter of Ikatan Keluarga Alumni STAN, connecting PKN STAN alumni who are studying, working, or on assignment across the United Kingdom.

We are a space for sharing information, strengthening bonds, and supporting one another through the academic and professional journey abroad.

🎓 Scholarship Support 📚 Academic Network 🇬🇧 Professional Life in the UK 🤝 Alumni Gatherings

Our members are active students and alumni from institutions across the UK.

Whether you're newly admitted or already settled in the UK, IKANAS UK is your network. This is a community that understands the unique experience of being a PKN STAN alumnus in the UK.
